No measured mission pack or real generated map tiles exist in `public/data/`.

Small PNG overviews are in [`previews/`](previews/). They preserve raster aspect ratios, use nearest-neighbor sampling, show nodata as transparent, and stretch each raster independently from its sampled 2nd to 98th percentile into grayscale. They are **non-operational visualizations**, not comparable elevation scales or thermal products. The sample elevation minima/maxima in the manifest are estimates from these overviews, not full-resolution extrema. No raw raster values were changed.
