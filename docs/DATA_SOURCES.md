# Data sources and provenance

The local CTX context pair has accompanying source notes in `ARES-DATA/01_OPTICAL/information.txt` and `ARES-DATA/02_ELEVATION/information.txt`. They identify USGS Astrogeology's 2020 Mars 2020 Terrain Relative Navigation CTX orthomosaic and DTM products, with source product pages, DOIs, process descriptions, and the requirement to cite the authors. Read the original [orthomosaic product page](https://astrogeology.usgs.gov/search/map/mars_2020_terrain_relative_navigation_context_camera_orthorectified_image_mosaic) and [DTM product page](https://astrogeology.usgs.gov/search/map/mars_2020_terrain_relative_navigation_ctx_dtm_mosaic) before redistribution or scientific claims. The copied local notes state 6 m optical and 20 m DTM resolution, but do not establish a license for every future use.

The high-resolution Central Jezero and Southwest Jezero GeoTIFFs contain a projected Mars CRS and `AREA_OR_POINT=Area`, but no embedded product identifier, acquisition date, author, vertical datum, license, or citation in their TIFF tags. `ARES-DATA/05_REFERENCES/LINKS.TXT` lists [Caltech Mars GIS](https://murray-lab.caltech.edu/Mars2020/index.html) and a [Caltech data record](https://data.caltech.edu/records/stqse-yay61) as possible leads. File identity and redistribution terms must be checked against an original download page or sidecar metadata; do not infer provenance merely from filenames or pixel resolution. Northern Fan sources remain unknown because the files are absent.

The science-point JSON contains per-record NASA/JPL source and image URLs and credits. These were checked for syntax only. Each factual claim and coordinate requires review against the linked original source, including whether the coordinate is a rover location, sample locality, reference point, or geological feature. Null coordinates should remain null until a defensible point location exists.

Synthetic WebP layers in `public/` and `src/data/demo-terrain.json` were generated for this prototype and are not measured planetary data. No local thermal raster suitable for co-registration was identified; the demo thermal WebP is illustrative.
