## Geographic integrity gate

The mission-pack WKT is Mars 2000 IAU sphere equirectangular, radius 3,396,190 m, central meridian 0°, standard parallel 0°. Submitted longitudes are east-positive planetocentric-style degrees. For this spherical CRS, `E = R × lonRadians` and `N = R × latRadians`; Rasterio's Mars-aware `transform` independently produced the same coordinates. Bounds are E 4,586,000–4,589,200 m and N 1,091,000–1,093,400 m. A point must be inside, have a valid DEM cell, and have adequate source verification before it can become a named routable preset.
