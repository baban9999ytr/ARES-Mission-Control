What is real in ARES v0.2?

Measured CTX study crop

Jezero optical mosaic and DEM data are processed into aligned WebP layers and a 80 × 60 height grid at 40 m cell spacing. Map coverage: projected E 4,586,000–4,589,200 m and N 1,091,000–1,093,400 m. Original optical sampling is 6 m/pixel; DEM sampling is 20 m/pixel.

Route computation

Eight-neighbour A* computes shortest grid distance or a terrain-preferred path with an illustrative slope-cost weighting. The displayed maximum slope is between adjacent 40 m sampled grid cells, not a maximum physical surface slope.

Separate synthetic demonstration

The optional demo mode uses procedurally generated height values, imagery, hazard zones and thermal colouring. No measured CTX thermal or verified hazard layer is provided.

Known scientific limits

ARES does not resolve rocks or sand at EVA scale, certify traversability, model life-support constraints, or calculate local Mars radiation exposure. User-selected points and example waypoints are not authenticated named geological targets. Outside-crop targets are not routed.
